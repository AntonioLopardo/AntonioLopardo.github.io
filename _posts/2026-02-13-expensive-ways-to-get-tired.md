So I signed up for a Half Ironman. 1.9 km of swimming, 90 km of cycling, and a half marathon to wrap things up — because apparently one sport wasn't enough of a way to suffer. Some people take up knitting. I chose to spend my weekends questioning my life choices while gasping for air in three different disciplines.

What follows is a collection of training sessions that, taken together, represent a significant investment of time, money, and dignity. Each one brought me marginally closer to race day and significantly closer to needing a nap.

---

## Running — "The cheapest sport (they said)"

Running is supposed to be the simple one. Just shoes and a road, right? Wrong. Shoes with carbon plates, a GPS watch that judges your VO2 max, energy gels that taste like flavoured regret — it adds up. But at least I get to do it *after* swimming and cycling, when my legs feel like they belong to someone else.
