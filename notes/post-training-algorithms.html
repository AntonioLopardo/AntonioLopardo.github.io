Notes · Overview

Post-training algorithms

Every method used to turn a base model into a deployed one, in eight families, each as a card: the objective in a line, what it fixes in its predecessor, and which of the ten reports ships it. The RLHF Book is the source for the classical entries; everything from 2025 on links its paper. Ten of them get the loss written out at the bottom, because that is where the field's disagreements actually live.

Fig 1 · Family map

AImitation and distillation

Learn from demonstrations — someone else's, your own filtered ones, or a teacher's token distributions.

SFT

— · book

Maximize log-likelihood of curated prompt–response pairs; token-level cross-entropy.

The stage every pipeline starts with. Loss normalization matters: Nemotron splits SFT into token-averaged then per-conversation-averaged stages because a single token-level pass wrecked long-input / short-output behaviour.

RLHF Book · ships in Nemotron

Rejection-sampling FT / STaR / ReST-EM

2022–23 · Zelikman; Google

Sample K responses, keep those a verifier accepts, SFT on them; iterate.

The cheapest on-policy method: no gradient through rewards, just filtered self-samples. Expert iteration under another name. Diversity and difficulty are not pushed unless you add them.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Llama-Nemotron GLM-5

Off-policy distillation

2015→ · Hinton; Phi-4; Llama-Nemotron

SFT on a stronger teacher's traces.

Fast and dominant in 2025 reports; the student's ceiling is the teacher. Llama-Nemotron: distillation caps Ultra at DeepSeek-R1, RL is what lets it pass the teacher. Phi-4: prompt selection (“teachable”) mattered more than trace volume.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Phi-4 Llama-Nemotron OLMo 3

GKD (generalized knowledge distillation)

2023 · Google DeepMind

Distill on student-generated sequences, per-token, with reverse KL or JSD instead of forward KL on teacher data.

The paper that made distillation on-policy: fixes train/inference mismatch and lets the student learn what to do in its own states. Everything called OPD descends from it.

arXiv · alphaXiv

On-policy distillation (OPD)

2025 · Qwen3; Thinking Machines

Student samples; reward per token = sg[log π_T(y_t) − log π_θ(y_t)]; optimize with the RL machinery.

Dense per-token signal at RL's exploration cost — Thinking Machines report reasoning parity with RL at ~1/10 the GPU hours. Qwen3 uses it for strong-to-weak after an off-policy phase.

Thinking Machines blog · ships in Qwen Kimi K3

Multi-teacher / full-vocabulary OPD

2026 · Kimi K3; DeepSeek V4

Σ_i w_i · KL(π_θ ‖ π_Ei) on student samples, one teacher per domain (and per effort level).

Replaces the mixed RL stage: train specialists separately, merge by distillation. DeepSeek V4 argues the per-token log-ratio estimate is high-variance and computes the KL over the full vocabulary instead; Kimi keeps the per-token form and found top-k variants gave no gain.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in DeepSeek V4 Kimi K3

Cross-stage OPD

2026 · GLM-5

Same objective, but the teachers are the model's own final checkpoints from earlier stages (SFT, reasoning RL, general RL).

Aimed at cumulative forgetting across sequential RL objectives rather than at merging domains. Group size 1, batch 1024 — no group needed since the teacher gap is the advantage.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in GLM-5

Delta learning

2025 · Ai2 (Geng et al.)

Preference-tune on pairs whose *difference* is informative even when both sides are weak: chosen from a stronger model, rejected from a much weaker one.

SFT on Qwen3-32B traces hurt OLMo 3; pairing them against Qwen3-0.6B traces turned the same data into a gain. The signal is the delta, not the absolute quality.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

PivotRL

2026 · NVIDIA

Turn-level RL over offline SFT expert trajectories: update only at ‘pivot’ turns where the policy is uncertain, reward similarity to the expert action.

A deliberate SFT/RL hybrid for agentic domains — SFT was cheap but degraded out-of-domain, end-to-end RL too expensive. No paper yet; described in the Nemotron 3 Super report.

Nemotron 3 Super §3.2.4 · ships in Nemotron

BReward and verification signals

What the optimizer is pointed at. Everything downstream is only as good as this.

Bradley–Terry reward model

2017→ · book

Train r_φ so that σ(r(y_w) − r(y_l)) matches human pairwise preferences.

The classic RLHF signal. Scalar, cheap to query, easy to over-optimize; the book's regularization chapter is mostly about this.

RLHF Book · ships in Llama-Nemotron

ORM vs PRM

2023 · OpenAI (Lightman et al.)

Outcome reward on the final answer vs process reward on each step.

PRMs give credit assignment but need step labels and are gameable; the 2025–26 frontier reports use outcome rewards almost exclusively and let long CoT do its own credit assignment.

arXiv · alphaXiv

Verifiable rewards (RLVR)

2024 · Ai2 Tülu 3; DeepSeek-R1

Replace the reward model with a checker: math answer match, unit tests, constraint verifiers.

The signal that made reasoning RL work. OLMo 3's verifier set — SymPy equivalence, tests on AWS Lambda, IF constraint functions, a Qwen3-32B judge for chat — is the concrete instance.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3 Phi-4

LLM-as-judge / GenRM

2023–24 · Zheng et al.; Zhang et al.

A language model scores or compares responses, optionally with chain-of-thought before the verdict.

Extends RL to non-verifiable domains. Nemotron trains a principle-following GenRM from Qwen3-235B-Thinking; Kimi's runs a mandatory rubric protocol as a tournament of binary comparisons.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Nemotron Kimi K3

Rubrics as rewards

2025 · Scale AI et al.

Judge against a per-prompt checklist rather than a holistic score.

Makes GenRM scoring auditable and harder to game; DeepSeek V4's hard-to-verify RL data is rubric-guided.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in DeepSeek V4

Actor-as-judge

2026 · DeepSeek V4

No separate reward model: apply RL to the policy's own judging, so generating and evaluating are trained jointly.

The verifier slot answered with ‘the policy itself’. Claimed to need minimal human annotation; the self-labelling risk R-Zero fell into, taken on purpose with rubrics as grounding.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in DeepSeek V4

WARM (weight-averaged reward models)

2024 · Google DeepMind

Average several fine-tuned RMs' weights for a more robust, less hackable reward.

Part of Gemma's RL stack; cheap insurance against reward hacking.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Gemma 4

Length-aware and budget rewards

2025–26 · Phi-4; DAPO; Kimi

Shape reward by output length: penalize overlong correct answers, reward longer thinking when wrong, or hard −1 beyond a per-problem budget τ·b₀.

How reasoning effort becomes a trained property. Phi-4's cosine schedule (25,600 / 3,702-token thresholds), DAPO's soft overlong penalty, Kimi K3's annealed τ giving low / high / max experts.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Phi-4 Kimi K3

CDirect alignment (offline)

Skip the reward model and the rollouts: optimize the policy on a fixed dataset of preferences or scored samples.

DPO

2023 · Stanford

Closed-form of KL-regularized RLHF: logistic loss on the implicit reward β·log π_θ/π_ref between chosen and rejected.

The default second stage of 2024–25. Failure modes the successors fix: pushes down both completions' likelihoods, relative-only signal, length bias.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

IPO

2023 · Google DeepMind

Regress the implicit reward gap onto a constant 1/(2β) instead of a logistic loss.

Removes DPO's tendency to over-optimize deterministic preferences.

arXiv · alphaXiv

KTO

2024 · Contextual AI

Prospect-theory loss on unpaired ‘good’ / ‘bad’ examples — no pairs needed.

Useful when you have thumbs-up/down but not comparisons.

arXiv · alphaXiv

ORPO

2024 · KAIST

Odds-ratio penalty added to the SFT loss; no reference model.

One stage instead of SFT → DPO; cheaper, slightly weaker at scale.

arXiv · alphaXiv

SimPO

2024 · Princeton

Length-normalized implicit reward with a target margin; no reference model.

Fixes DPO's length bias directly in the objective.

arXiv · alphaXiv

APO (anchored)

2024 · Contextual AI

Contrastive objectives that specify whether the chosen response should rise, the rejected fall, or both, relative to the model's current outputs.

SmolLM3's preference stage; the ‘which side moves’ distinction matters when the data is on- vs off-policy.

arXiv · alphaXiv

RPO (reward-aware)

2024–25 · NVIDIA

Regress the implicit reward gap onto the reward model's actual gap, not just the binary order.

Prevents DPO overfitting when a good RM exists; Llama-Nemotron's online RPO lifted Arena Hard 69 → 88.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Llama-Nemotron

QRPO (quantile reward)

2025 · EPFL / Apertus

Regress β·log π_θ/π_ref onto a quantile-transformed absolute reward, for which the KL-regularized optimum's partition function is exact.

Pointwise rewards (RM scores, judge scores, rankings) instead of pairs, offline, DAA-stable. Apertus's alignment stage.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Apertus

Iterative / online DPO

2024 · many

Regenerate pairs from the current policy each round, judge them, DPO again.

Closes most of the gap to RL for chat quality; OLMo 3 Instruct's GPT-judged delta-aware pairs are this with a ‘pick the worst as rejected’ twist.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

DPolicy gradient, critic-free

Sample a group per prompt, use the group as the baseline, no value network. Where almost all 2025–26 movement is.

REINFORCE

1992 · Williams

∇J = E[(r − b)·∇log π(y|x)]; a baseline b reduces variance.

Everything below is REINFORCE with a better baseline, a trust region, or both.

RLHF Book

ReMax

2023 · HKUST

Baseline = reward of the greedy decode of the same prompt.

One extra greedy sample per prompt instead of a critic.

arXiv · alphaXiv

RLOO

2024 · Cohere

Baseline for sample k = mean reward of the other K−1 samples.

Unbiased, critic-free, and what Llama-Nemotron uses for its IF stage.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Llama-Nemotron

REINFORCE++

2025 · OpenRLHF

REINFORCE with PPO's token-level KL penalty and reward normalization, no critic.

Cheaper than PPO, steadier than vanilla REINFORCE; a reasonable baseline in ablations.

arXiv · alphaXiv

GRPO

2024 · DeepSeek

PPO-style clipped ratio per token; advantage = (R_i − mean_G) / std_G broadcast to all tokens; KL term in the loss.

The consensus name. The successors each remove one of its choices.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Phi-4 Llama-Nemotron OLMo 3

Dr. GRPO

2025 · Sea AI Lab

GRPO minus 1/|o_i| and minus /std_G.

Removes a length bias (long wrong answers get less penalty per token) and a difficulty bias (easy/hard prompts get inflated advantages). OLMo 3 and SPICE adopt it.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

DAPO

2025 · ByteDance Seed

Clip-higher (ε_high 0.28), dynamic sampling, token-level loss, overlong shaping, no KL.

The open recipe most 2026 GRPOs are built on. Qwen2.5-32B-Base → 50 on AIME 2024.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3 GLM-5

GSPO

2025 · Qwen

Importance ratio and clipping at the sequence level: s_i = (π_θ(y_i)/π_old(y_i))^{1/|y_i|}.

Token ratios are noisy and blow up for MoE; sequence-level matching of reward and ratio fixed Qwen3's RL stability. Clip range becomes tiny (~3–4e-4).

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Qwen

GMPO

2025 · ICLR 2026

Maximize the geometric rather than arithmetic mean of token-level rewards.

Outlier tokens with extreme ratios stop dominating; wider clip ranges become safe.

arXiv · alphaXiv

SAPO

2025 · Qwen

Replace hard clipping with a smooth temperature-controlled gate on off-policy tokens.

Sequence-coherent like GSPO, token-adaptive like GRPO; used in Qwen3-VL.

arXiv · alphaXiv

CISPO

2025 · MiniMax

Clip the importance weight, detach it, and keep the plain REINFORCE gradient for every token.

PPO clipping silently drops the rare ‘fork’ tokens (however, wait, …) that carry reasoning; CISPO keeps their gradient. MiniMax-M1's full RL in 3 weeks on 512 H800s.

arXiv · alphaXiv

EPolicy gradient, with a critic

The value network is not dead; it just costs more than most labs want to pay.

PPO

2017 · OpenAI

Clipped surrogate with GAE advantages from a learned value function; token-level KL to a reference.

The original RLHF optimizer. Value model of policy size, four models in memory, GAE tuning — the cost GRPO was invented to avoid.

arXiv · alphaXiv

VAPO

2025 · ByteDance Seed

PPO plus: value pretraining, decoupled GAE (λ=1 for the critic, length-adaptive λ for the policy), clip-higher, token-level loss, positive-example LM loss, group sampling.

The counter-argument: 60.4 on AIME 2024 from Qwen2.5-32B, ten points over DAPO, with no training collapses. Value-based has the higher ceiling if you can afford the critic.

arXiv · alphaXiv

FOff-policy and asynchrony corrections

2026's new layer: the sampler and the learner are different programs, different precisions, and often different model versions.

Truncated / masked importance sampling

2025 · Yao et al.

Multiply the loss by min(π_learner/π_sampler, C) (TIS), or mask tokens whose ratio leaves [1/C, C] (MIS).

The ‘your framework is secretly off-policy’ result: vLLM vs trainer logprobs differ enough to destabilize training. OLMo 3 uses truncated IS with C≈2; Nemotron masks the ratio.

Yao et al. blog · ships in OLMo 3 Nemotron

IcePop

2025 · Ant Group (Ring-1T)

Zero out tokens whose train/infer ratio is outside [1/β, β] and clip the rest; β = 2 in GLM-5.

Made RL at a trillion parameters stable; GLM-5 adopts it minus the KL term for its synchronous reasoning RL.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in GLM-5

Direct double-sided IS

2026 · GLM-5

Ratio against the rollout log-probs directly (no stored old policy), mask outside [1−ε_l, 1+ε_h].

For fully async agentic RL where the rollout engine updates mid-trajectory and tracking π_old is infeasible. Plus: drop samples whose oldest rollout version is too stale.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in GLM-5

VCPO (Stable Asynchrony)

2026 · MIT Han Lab

Control the variance of importance weights under high policy lag instead of truncating them.

Stale rollouts give heavy-tailed weights; a few trajectories dominate. Matches synchronous accuracy 2.5× faster where TIS collapsed.

arXiv · alphaXiv

Partial rollouts

2025 · Kimi k1.5 → K3

Pause generation when a fraction λ of the N×K trajectories finish; resume the stragglers next iteration.

Trajectories span model versions by design; a per-token regularizer keeps the stale data usable. The reason Kimi can run million-token agentic RL at all.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Kimi K3

Routing / indexer replay

2025–26 · R3; GLM-5

Record the MoE routing (or sparse-attention top-k) chosen at rollout and replay it in training.

For MoE and DSA, the discrete selection is itself a train/infer mismatch. GLM-5 found a deterministic torch.topk and a frozen indexer were enough; non-deterministic kernels collapsed RL in a few steps.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in GLM-5

GSampling and curriculum machinery

Which prompts, which groups, and how much entropy — the knobs that decide whether any of the above learns anything.

Difficulty filtering

2025 · everyone

Drop prompts the current model already solves (or never solves) before RL; sort the rest by pass rate.

Same object, five names: Phi-4 ‘teachable’, OLMo 3's 62.5% cutoff, Llama-Nemotron's 0.75 cutoff with a Gaussian curriculum, Nemotron's ‘drop what SFT always solves’, GLM-5's ‘hard for 4.7 but solvable by GPT-5.2’.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Phi-4 OLMo 3 Llama-Nemotron

Dynamic sampling → active sampling

2025 · DAPO; OLMo 3

Refill the batch with prompts whose group reward has non-zero variance.

Vanilla GRPO's effective batch shrinks as accuracy rises; DAPO oversamples 3×, OlmoRL's async loop refills continuously at lower cost.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

Clip-higher and entropy control

2025 · DAPO; CE-GPPO; Cui et al.

Raise ε_high so low-probability tokens can grow; or preserve gradients from clipped tokens with a bounded coefficient.

Entropy collapse is the characteristic failure of reasoning RL; ‘The Entropy Mechanism’ shows performance is predictable from entropy and proposes covariance-based clipping.

arXiv · alphaXiv

Multi-environment joint training

2026 · Nemotron 3 Super

Train one policy on all environments at once rather than sequentially.

Nemotron's strongest transferable claim: 21 environments jointly is stable, single-environment RL regresses everything else. GLM-5 stages by rollout economics instead.

Nemotron 3 Super §3.2.1 · ships in Nemotron GLM-5

Self-play task generation

2022–25 · STaR → SPICE

Let a proposer manufacture the curriculum at the solver's 50% boundary.

Has its own note.

HOdds and ends worth a card

Not a family; things that show up in the reports and deserve a name.

BOND (best-of-N distillation)

2024 · Google DeepMind

Distill the best-of-N sampling policy into the model via Jeffreys divergence.

Gets best-of-N quality at single-sample cost; part of the Gemma RL stack.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Gemma 4

WARP (weight-averaged rewarded policies)

2024 · Google DeepMind

EMA anchors, spherical interpolation of independently RL'd policies, interpolate toward init.

Model merging as regularization inside RLHF; the other half of Gemma's stack.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in Gemma 4

Model souping

2022 · Wortsman et al.

Linearly average checkpoints.

OLMo 3's SFT checkpoint is a soup of two learning rates. Free, and it keeps working.

arXiv · alphaXiv · ships in OLMo 3

MTP healing

2026 · Nemotron 3 Super

After RL, retrain only the multi-token-prediction heads with the trunk frozen.

RL moves the trunk and the draft's acceptance rate collapses. Has its own note.

The math, for the ten that matter

Written to be diffed against each other. Names in bold in the notes are the term that changed.

GRPO

DeepSeek 2024
J = E[ 1/G Σ_i 1/|o_i| Σ_t min( r_{i,t} Â_i , clip(r_{i,t}, 1−ε, 1+ε) Â_i ) ] − β·KL(π_θ ‖ π_ref)
r_{i,t} = π_θ(o_{i,t} | x, o_{i,<t}) / π_old(o_{i,t} | x, o_{i,<t})
Â_i = ( R_i − mean(R_1..R_G) ) / std(R_1..R_G) // one advantage per sequence, broadcast to its tokens

Three choices the successors attack: the 1/|o_i| per-sequence averaging, the /std in the advantage, and the symmetric clip.

Dr. GRPO

Sea AI Lab 2025
J = E[ 1/G Σ_i Σ_t min( r_{i,t} Â_i , clip(·) Â_i ) ] // 1/|o_i| removed (or a constant)
Â_i = R_i − mean(R_1..R_G) // /std removed

Without 1/|o_i|, a long wrong answer is penalized in proportion to its length — the bias that made GRPO responses grow. Without /std, near-solved and near-impossible prompts stop getting inflated advantages.

DAPO

ByteDance 2025
J = E[ 1/Σ_i|o_i| Σ_i Σ_t min( r_{i,t} Â_i , clip(r_{i,t}, 1−ε_low, 1+ε_high) Â_i ) ] // token-level, no KL
ε_low = 0.20, ε_high = 0.28 // clip-higher
batch = { x : 0 < #correct(x) < G } // dynamic sampling; + soft overlong penalty

Same estimator as Dr. GRPO on the normalization, plus an asymmetric trust region so rare tokens can gain probability, and a batch that never contains zero-gradient groups.

GSPO

Qwen 2025
s_i = ( π_θ(y_i | x) / π_old(y_i | x) )^{1/|y_i|} // sequence ratio, length-normalized
J = E[ 1/G Σ_i min( s_i Â_i , clip(s_i, 1−ε, 1+ε) Â_i ) ] // clip and optimize whole sequences
ε ≈ 3e-4 … 4e-4 // tiny, because s_i lives near 1

The reward is per sequence, so the ratio is made per sequence too. Token ratios accumulate noise over long outputs and, in MoE models, are corrupted by expert-routing flips between rollout and training; the sequence ratio averages that out.

CISPO

MiniMax 2025
J = E[ 1/Σ_i|o_i| Σ_i Σ_t sg( min(r_{i,t}, ε_IS) ) · Â_i · log π_θ(o_{i,t} | x, o_{i,<t}) ]
// the importance weight is clipped and detached; the gradient itself is never clipped away

PPO/GRPO clipping zeroes the gradient of tokens whose ratio left the trust region — which are exactly the low-probability ‘fork’ tokens a reasoning model needs to raise. CISPO keeps every token's REINFORCE gradient and only bounds the weight.

VAPO

ByteDance 2025
policy: PPO clipped objective, token-level, ε_high raised, + positive-example NLL term
critic: pretrained on fixed-policy rollouts, GAE with λ_critic = 1; policy uses λ_policy = 1 − 1/(α·ℓ)
// length-adaptive λ: long chains get a longer credit-assignment horizon

Every ingredient exists elsewhere; the claim is that a well-trained value model plus these fixes beats every critic-free method at long CoT. Ten AIME points over DAPO on the same base.

Truncated / masked IS · IcePop

Yao et al. 2025 · Ring-1T 2025 · GLM-5 2026
ρ_t = π_learner(y_t | ·) / π_sampler(y_t | ·) // trainer vs inference engine, same weights, different numerics
TIS: loss_t ← min(ρ_t, C) · loss_t (C ≈ 2)
IcePop / MIS: loss_t ← 0 if ρ_t ∉ [1/β, β] else ρ_t · loss_t (β = 2)

Not a policy-improvement trick — a correction for the fact that the rollout engine is a different program. Async training adds a second ratio (current vs stale policy); GLM-5 folds both into one ratio against the rollout log-probs and masks outside [1−ε_l, 1+ε_h].

On-policy distillation

GKD 2023 · Qwen3 / Thinking Machines 2025 · DeepSeek V4 2026
L_OPD = E_{y ~ π_θ} Σ_t KL( π_θ(· | x, y_<t) ‖ π_T(· | x, y_<t) ) // reverse KL on the student's own samples
RL-style estimate: Â_t = sg[ log π_T(y_t | ·) − log π_θ(y_t | ·) ] // one number per token, plug into the policy loss
full-vocabulary form (DeepSeek V4): compute the KL over all |V| logits instead of the sampled token

Dense reward from a teacher instead of sparse reward from a verifier, on the student's own trajectories. Multi-teacher versions weight Σ_i w_i KL(π_θ ‖ π_Ei) so the student matches whichever expert owns the prompt.

DPO

Stanford 2023
L = −E log σ( β [ log π_θ(y_w|x)/π_ref(y_w|x) − log π_θ(y_l|x)/π_ref(y_l|x) ] )
// the KL-regularized RLHF optimum π* ∝ π_ref · exp(r/β) rewritten so the reward is implicit

Relative signal only, offline. Successors differ in what they anchor to (APO), whether they need π_ref (SimPO, ORPO), whether pairs are needed (KTO), and whether the RM's actual gap is used (RPO).

QRPO

EPFL 2025
π*(y|x) = π_ref(y|x) · exp( r(x,y)/β ) / Z(x) // same optimum as DPO starts from
r_q(x,y) = quantile of r(x,y) under y ~ π_ref(·|x), transformed so Z(x) has a closed form
L = ( β · log π_θ(y|x)/π_ref(y|x) − r_q(x,y) + β·log Z(x) )² // pointwise regression, no pairs

Absolute rewards (RM scores, judge scores, rankings) used offline with DAA stability, and without DPO's habit of pushing both completions down. The Apertus alignment stage.
