Verifiable science data and environments

Ten papers on scalable, verifiable science data and environments for the Apertus 2 STEM climb. Nine are from the last twelve months; only MegaScience (July 2025) is older. Grouped by where the verifiable signal comes from.

Literature-mined QA

Papers and textbooks become multiple-choice or short-answer questions. Cheap, broad, verifiable by string match; quality rests on the filtering.

UC Santa BarbaraJanuary 2026arxiv 2601.05567
data released

WildSciScientific reasoning from in-the-wild literature

  • 56K MCQ from Nature Communications papers, 9 disciplines, 26 subdomains; LLM generation, refinement with expanded options, model voting as filter
  • GRPO on Qwen 1.5B and 3B with verifiable MCQ reward; +7.3 avg on GPQA-Aug, SuperGPQA, MMLU-Pro at 1.5B
  • Template for mining Swiss and EU open-access corpora into RL prompts
U Chicago, ArgonneApril 2026arxiv 2604.03506
model on the Hub

BioAlchemyBiological literature into RL training data

  • 345K verifiable biology QA pairs mined from research text, aligned to current topic distributions
  • BioAlchemist-8B: +9.1% over its base on biology benchmarks
  • Same recipe as WildSci at domain depth; shows a single field can carry a climb
SJTU, GAIRJuly 2025arxiv 2507.16812
CC BY-NC-SA

MegaSciencePost-training datasets for science reasoning

  • 1.25M instances; 651K from 12.8K university textbooks across 7 disciplines, plus ablated public sets
  • SFT on Llama 3.1, Qwen2.5, Qwen3 beats the official instruct models on 15 benchmarks
  • Licence blocks it for Apertus training; useful as the eval and ablation reference

Simulators and procedural generators

Answers are computed, not annotated. Unlimited supply and controllable difficulty; the question is transfer to real problems.

CMU, LambdaApril 2026arxiv 2604.11805
codemodels 3B to 30B

Sim2ReasonPhysics Olympiad via RL on physics simulators

  • Random MuJoCo scenes through a DSL; numeric, reverse and symbolic questions with answers from running the simulation
  • Trained only on simulated data: IPhO mechanics +5.4 at 32B, +7.5 at 3B; up to +17.9 on JEEBench
  • The clearest sim-to-real result; the pattern to copy for chemistry and circuits
Inria, Univ. LilleMarch 2026arxiv 2603.02208
MIT5B + 2B tokens

Reasoning CoreProcedural data for symbolic pre- and post-training

  • Generators for PDDL planning, first-order logic, CFG parsing, causal reasoning over Bayesian nets, symbolic equations; external solvers verify, difficulty is continuous
  • Small-model pretraining mixes improve PlatinumBench without hurting LM loss
  • Follow-up (2608.05148): semantic validity alone does not guarantee training utility
  • European Reasoning Gym; licence-clean filler for the climb prompt pool

Executable environments

Agentic science: the model writes and runs code against real data, a hidden verifier grades. Closer to the tools/SWE climb than to a reasoning climb; the transferable idea is verifier synthesis.

Ohio State, Wisconsin, CiscoApril 2026arxiv 2604.27977
envs, workflow, trajectories, models

D3-GymVerifiable environments for data-driven discovery

  • 565 tasks from 239 real scientific repositories: bioinformatics, computational chemistry, GIS, psychology
  • Evaluation scripts synthesised plan-then-code, 87.5% agreement with human pass/fail
  • Rejection-sampled trajectories lift Qwen3-32B by +7.8 on ScienceAgentBench
  • The anchor of the environment family; the verifier-synthesis step is reusable on its own
Shanghai AI LabJuly 2026arxiv 2607.28990
release not stated

SciDisco / SciThèqueScaling discovery environments for turn-level agentic RL

  • 1,686 environments plus 504 stress variants over licence-tracked public data (UCI, OpenML, FRED, UniProt, CDC, USGS); tabular 61%, time series 17%, spatial 12%
  • Each environment carries a hypothesis, the data, a hidden evidence DAG and a verifier; DiscoPO gives turn-level credit
  • SciDisco-14B from Qwen3-14B: DiscoveryBench 35.2 vs Claude Sonnet 4 at 23.2
  • D3-Gym at 3× scale with licence tracking built in; nothing to download yet
Snowflake, UNCFebruary 2026, ICML 2026arxiv 2602.10090
code and environments

Agent World ModelSynthetic environments for agentic RL

  • LLM pipeline synthesises executable tool-use environments: scenario, tasks, database schema, interface, verification code
  • 1,000 environments, 35K tools, 10K tasks; GRPO on 526 of them takes Qwen3-8B from 53.8 to 65.9 on BFCLv3, gains on τ²-bench and MCP-Universe
  • Not science-specific; the environment-synthesis recipe the science gyms could borrow to scale past hand-built repos
ApodexAugust 2026arxiv 2608.24979
eval only97 of 300 released

FrontierChallengeEvaluating scientific workflow completion

  • 300 end-to-end lab workflows: quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics, materials, analytical chemistry, life science, electrochemistry; fixed inputs, required deliverables
  • Best configurations pass 20 of 97; 75.5% of failing Claude Code runs still claimed completion
  • The held-out target for the environment family, and a warning about self-reported success

Tooling

Generation infrastructure rather than a dataset.

NVIDIA15 September 2026arxiv 2609.17699
open source

NeMo Data DesignerExtensible framework for synthetic data generation

  • Declarative config: each field is a column with a generation strategy; samplers control diversity before any model call; LLM steps, validators and judges as stages
  • Behind recent Nemotron datasets; worked example is 96.5K validated text-to-SQL rows from 300K candidates, BIRD 26.8 to 41.8
  • The pipeline Nemotron-SFT-Science-v2 style sets come from; rebuild the science recipe on Apertus-clean sources

Caveat for any GPQA claim above: HLE-Physics moved from 47 to 79 after expert regrading (arxiv 2609.13009), closed-ended physics evals are noisy.
